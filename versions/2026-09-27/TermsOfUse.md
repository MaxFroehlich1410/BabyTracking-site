## 10. Account deletion

Deletion is started in Settings or through “Delete cloud account and continue locally” and scheduled for seven days. Switching locally requires a separate confirmation. Deletion can be cancelled during that period after signing in again; cloud functions are blocked meanwhile. Choosing “Delete cloud account and continue locally” preserves care and moment data already on this device as a local copy. Cloud deletion does not remove this copy; it can be deleted separately in the app. After seven days, the account, profile, consent record, Apple authorisation and linked RevenueCat customer profile are removed.

For a one-member household, cloud entries and moment photos are removed. If other members remain, jointly maintained entries and photos remain for them; the deleted user’s creator attribution is removed and required administration is transferred. Content that should not remain should be deleted beforehand or raised with the provider.

Encrypted database and moment-photo backup archives are stored on a server operated by the provider in Germany and removed during the daily backup run after seven days. Previously backed-up data may therefore remain in encrypted backups after deletion from the active system until the corresponding archive rotation. Backup archives are used for recovery and are not accessible through the app.
