2. Service

BabyTrack supports private documentation of breast and bottle feeding, sleep, diapers, custom activities and moment photos, with analyses, Live Activities and optional reminders. Local mode keeps care and moment data on that device; BabyTrack does not provide its own cross-device backup for it. Cloud mode stores and syncs these contents, including photos, through Supabase with authorised household members.

The app is a documentation tool, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace a doctor, midwife or other qualified professional. Seek professional help for health concerns or emergencies.

Users must provide accurate details, use secure and unique credentials, protect their device and account, and promptly report suspected misuse. Invitation codes must be treated as access credentials and shared only with trusted people.

10. Account deletion

Deletion is started in Settings or through “Delete cloud account and continue locally” and scheduled for seven days. Switching locally requires a separate confirmation. Deletion can be cancelled during that period after signing in again; cloud functions are blocked meanwhile. Account-associated local data is cleared when deletion starts. After seven days, the account, profile, consent record, Apple authorisation and linked RevenueCat customer profile are removed.

For a one-member household, cloud entries and moment photos are removed. If other members remain, jointly maintained entries and photos remain for them; the deleted user’s creator attribution is removed and required administration is transferred. Content that should not remain should be deleted beforehand or raised with the provider.

Deleted cloud data may remain in an encrypted backup archive. Archives are removed from the active backup folder after up to seven days; unless permanently deleted sooner, iCloud Drive may then keep deleted files recoverable for up to 30 additional days. The archives are used solely for security and recovery. See the Privacy Policy for details.
